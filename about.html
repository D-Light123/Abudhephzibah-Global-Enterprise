About Us

📖 Our Story

Abudhephzibah Global Enterprise was founded with a simple mission: to provide quality household provisions at affordable prices to the people of Benin City and beyond. What started as a small provision shop has grown into a trusted neighborhood store serving hundreds of customers daily.

We understand the importance of having access to quality food items, cleaning products, and household essentials without breaking the bank. That's why we carefully select our products to ensure they meet high standards while remaining budget-friendly.

Over the years, we've built strong relationships with our customers based on trust, quality, and excellent service. Whether you're a student, a family, a restaurant owner, or running a small business, we're here to meet your provision needs.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality household provisions and essential items at affordable prices, ensuring every customer gets the best value for their money while maintaining excellent customer service.

👁️ Our Vision

To become the most trusted and preferred provision shop in Benin City, known for quality products, fair prices, and outstanding customer service that keeps people coming back.
